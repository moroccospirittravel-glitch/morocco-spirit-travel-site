Student trip

Five days from Marrakech to the Sahara and back, built for groups of students.

Itinerary · 5 days / 4 nights

Sahara student escape

Sleep in the dunes, ride camels at sunset and get lost in the souks of Marrakech. One route, five days, zero stress.

01

Day 1

Arrival in Marrakech

Marrakech

We meet you at the airport and take you to your riad. Settle in, then wander into the old town for a first taste of the city. Night in Marrakech.

Airport pickupRiad stay

Land at any hour, we adapt to your flight.

02

Day 2

Atlas, kasbahs and the Dadès

High Atlas · Aït Ben Haddou · Dadès

Cross the High Atlas by the Tizi n'Tichka pass with photo stops, then explore Aït Ben Haddou, a UNESCO clay kasbah and a favourite film set. Follow the Road of 1,000 Kasbahs to the Dadès Valley for dinner and the night.

2,260 m passUNESCO site

A long scenic drive, so we keep the stops short and the playlist long.

03

Day 3

Todra Gorges and the dunes

Todra · Merzouga · Erg Chebbi

Walk into the Todra Gorges, where the walls climb over 300 m, then drive on to Merzouga. Trade the car for camels and ride to camp at sunset. Mint tea, dinner, drums and a sky full of stars.

Camel rideDesert campStargazing

Optional: sandboarding, quads or buggies (extra cost).

04

Day 4

Sunrise and the road back

Erg Chebbi → Marrakech

Rise early if you want to catch the sunrise over the dunes. After breakfast we head back to Marrakech through the Atlas, arriving in the evening. Night in Marrakech.

Dune sunriseScenic drive

Prefer to skip the long drive? Ask us about a domestic flight from Errachidia.

05

Day 5

Marrakech medina

Marrakech medina

A guided morning through the medina: the Koutoubia minaret, Bahia Palace and the souks, finishing at Jemaa el-Fna square. Then we drop you at the airport for your flight home.

Guided tourSouksJemaa el-Fna

Early flight? We will get you there with time to spare.

FAQ

Good to know

Can I fly back instead of driving?

Yes. A domestic flight from Errachidia to Marrakech can replace the long drive on day 4, subject to availability. Ask us and we will check the options.

Is this trip only for students?

It is designed for student groups, but anyone who likes the route is welcome. Tell us your group size and we will adapt the plan.

Can the itinerary be changed?

Of course. Add a night in the desert, shorten it to 4 days, or pick hotel categories from simple to luxury. We build it around your group.

What should we bring?

Comfortable shoes, a light scarf for the sun and wind, a warm layer for desert nights and a reusable water bottle.

Why Morocco with family

One trip, every age

Morocco is a country kids remember: riding a camel, sliding down a dune, bargaining in the souks and sleeping in a tent under a sky full of stars. For parents it is the chance to slow down and actually be together.

Every family trip is private. We plan the route, the driving hours and the stops around the youngest and the oldest in your group, so nobody is rushed and nobody is bored.

  • Private vehicle and a local driver-guide who travels only with your family
  • Short driving days with plenty of stops for rest, food and photos
  • Activities that work for kids and grown-ups: camel rides, sandboarding, cooking, medina walks
  • Family rooms or connecting rooms in riads, hotels and desert camps
  • Meals adapted to your family, just tell us what the little ones eat

Made around your family

Every family is different

01

Built around your ages

A family with a toddler does not travel like one with teenagers. We start from the ages in your group and the pace you can really keep, then plan the distances around it: shorter drives, regular stops and the busiest activities in the morning, when everyone has the most energy.

02

For the children

A camel ride at sunset, sandboarding down a dune, a cooking class, a wander through the souks. Things to touch, try and do, not just monuments to look at from a distance.

03

For the parents

Slow mornings, mint tea on a terrace, the medina at your own pace and meals with no clock to beat. Some afternoons we simply leave free.

04

Where you sleep

Family rooms or connecting rooms in riads and hotels, and a private tent for your night in the desert. Tell us how many you are and we match the rooms to your group.

05

Someone to talk to

Message us on WhatsApp before and during your trip. Your driver-guide stays with your family from the first day to the last, so there is always someone who knows your plan.

Tailor-made

Nothing here is a fixed package. Share the ages, the number of days and what your family enjoys, and we shape the route from there, rest days included.

FAQ

Good to know

Can we travel with babies or toddlers?

Yes. Tell us their ages and we will adapt the pace, the rest stops and the places you stay. Ask us about child seats and other needs when you request your quote.

Is the trip private?

Yes. Family trips are private journeys: only your group, your driver-guide and your own schedule.

Can we change the itinerary?

Of course. Add a night, remove a long drive, swap a city for a beach or a rest day. We build it around your family.

Which season is best?

Spring and autumn are the most comfortable for most families. Summer is hot inland and winter nights in the desert are cold, so we plan accordingly.

Car rental with driver

A comfortable vehicle, a local driver and all of Morocco at your own pace.

When you book a car with a driver, you get more than a ride. Our Moroccan drivers know the places you are visiting: they point out hidden corners, share a bit of history and culture, and give practical tips to make your holiday better. They speak the local languages and understand local customs, so they bridge any language gap and help you connect with the people you meet, which makes the whole trip feel more authentic.

With a car and driver at your disposal, the schedule is yours. Visit the big sights or head off the beaten track to find quieter places, and your driver shapes each day around your tastes and interests. That freedom lets you get the most out of Morocco and build a trip that is truly your own.

We quote net rates in EUR or USD. You add your own commission or margin before selling to your client.

What are the payment terms?

A deposit confirms the booking and the balance is due before arrival. Exact terms are in your rate agreement.

It depends on the season and the hotels involved. We state it clearly in every quote.

Air-conditioned cars, vans and minibuses with experienced drivers, plus licensed guides in English, French, Spanish and more on request.
